Kitchen & Bath Remodels

Remodels are where craftsmanship shows. We work shoulder-to-shoulder with homeowners, designers, and builders to rough-in and finish kitchens and baths that look flawless and last for decades.

A remodel is where plumbing craftsmanship becomes visible. The right fixtures, set perfectly, in exactly the right place — that's what turns a renovation into the room you've been picturing. We work hand-in-hand with homeowners, designers, and builders to make it happen.

Kitchens that work beautifully

Farmhouse sinks, pot fillers, filtered water taps, prep sinks, and the behind-the-wall plumbing that supports it all — installed so the finished kitchen looks and performs exactly as designed.

Bathrooms worth lingering in

Freestanding tubs, curbless showers, steam systems, wall-mounted fixtures, and heated details. We handle the relocation and rough-in that make ambitious bathroom designs possible.

Designed to match the vision

We coordinate closely with your designer or builder, reading plans carefully and flagging issues early so the plumbing supports the design instead of compromising it.

What we handle

  • Kitchen remodels
  • Primary & guest bath remodels
  • Freestanding tubs & steam showers
