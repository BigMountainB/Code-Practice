Brand Identity

The Davis Plumbing mark.

A timeless identity rooted in family heritage and the plumbing trade — heraldic styling, copper and brass, and a cross woven naturally into the form. Three concepts, one family.

Three Concepts

One family, three expressions.

Each concept combines the Davis family heritage with the craftsmanship of the trade — a modern take on the traditional coat of arms.

Concept 1

The Family Crest

A modernized heraldic shield with a copper riser and valve forming a subtle cross, brass border, and a wordmark banner. The fullest expression of the family's heritage.

Best for: Website, letterhead, signage, framed shop print.

Concept 2

The Mark

A clean shield monogram — a serif “D” with a copper riser — stripped to its essentials. Reads clearly at any size.

Best for: Truck wraps, hats, embroidery, social avatars, favicons.

Concept 3

Heraldic Luxury

A refined slim emblem paired with an elegant serif wordmark and fine gold rules — positioned like a luxury custom-home builder.

Best for: Proposals, premium print, high-end marketing.
