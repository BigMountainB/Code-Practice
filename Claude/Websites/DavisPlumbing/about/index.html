How We Started

It began with one man, a truck, and a reputation to protect.

Ernie Davis spent more than 30 years learning the plumbing trade the way it should be learned — on job sites, under experienced hands, doing the work until it was second nature. When he founded Davis Plumbing, he wasn't trying to build the biggest company in Washington. He was trying to build the most trusted one.

That meant showing up when he said he would. It meant charging a fair price and doing the job right, even when no one was watching. And it meant treating every home — whether a modest remodel or a multi-million-dollar custom build — with the same respect and craftsmanship.

Word travels in this business. One clean rough-in leads to another. One honest estimate earns a customer for life. Decades later, much of our work still comes from the builders, designers, and families who trusted us early — and never had a reason to call anyone else.

Our Commitment to Quality

Craftsmanship isn't a feature. It's the whole point.

Anyone can connect a pipe. Doing it so it's clean, accessible, correct, and built to outlast the home around it — that's the difference between a plumber and a craftsman. We've spent decades on the craftsman side of that line, and we don't intend to cross back.

“We'd rather earn a customer for the next twenty years than a job for the next twenty minutes.”

On a custom home, our rough-in is laid out with the same care a finish carpenter brings to trim. Our fixtures are set level, tight, and flawless. We coordinate with builders and designers so the plumbing supports the vision instead of fighting it. And when the inspector arrives, our work is ready — because we build it right the first time.
